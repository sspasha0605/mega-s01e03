0002 · Compare causes before merging reports

owner Codex with the MEGA student proposed reviewed 2026-10-30 · 0 days ago

Context

Decision 0001 groups by normalized subject. The #4471 investigation finds distinct parser and form-hint causes behind similar subjects. The supplied classifier and DedupeExplorer already distinguish these causes using tenant locale, form version and report channel.

Proposed decision

Read each report, deduplicate IDs, resolve its tenant, and compare the cause before merging. Matching subjects are a search aid, not proof. Different subjects do not prevent a merge when evidence establishes the same cause. Unknown tenants stay unresolved until investigated. Keep one-day observations visible as uncertainty instead of inventing a confirmed cause.

Consequences

Triage needs more context than a subject comparison, but engineering receives separate fixes with auditable counts. The desk must approve this proposal before it supersedes accepted decision 0001; that accepted record remains unchanged.
